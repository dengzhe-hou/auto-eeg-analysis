Auditory and visual responses in the repaired N100 example. (a) Condition means over the six selected ROI electrodes. (b) Auditory minus visual mean. Shading around the curves in a and b is ±1 SEM across 61 complete stimulus cycles; the difference SEM uses the within-cycle differences. Grey backgrounds show the planned 80–150 ms analysis window, not significance at individual time points. (c) Measured sensor locations and the six intended 10–20 targets, projected onto the x–y plane of head coordinates. Lines connect each selected electrode with its target; distances in this projection are not the three-dimensional matching distances. C1: minimum cluster p = 0.0004; 1 clusters pass the cluster-level threshold p < 0.05. Cluster-level inference does not localize significance to each time point or electrode. This is a single-participant analysis.
