# Statistical choices that need the approved plan

Read this reference when helping formulate a plan, not to replace a resolved
specification during execution. Preserve the approved primary analysis and all
results. The options below are conditional proposals.

- Fixed cluster-forming thresholds and TFCE are different procedures. Record
  the chosen method's actual parameters. Additional threshold-sensitivity tests
  are approved planned or labelled exploratory analyses, not automatic reruns.
- Arithmetic and trimmed means summarize differently. A trimmed-mean proposal
  needs an approved trimming fraction, observation unit, inference and interval.
  It does not automatically replace an arithmetic mean after seeing outliers or
  an unfavorable result. Participant exclusion also follows the approved policy.
- SEM, normal-approximation CI, Student-t CI and bootstrap CI are distinct.
  Record the chosen SD convention, confidence level, bootstrap method/count/seed
  where applicable, and the actual aggregation unit.
- A descriptive interval for a fixed ROI/window is not a cluster-corrected
  hypothesis test for that scalar mean. Preserve the test target in reporting.

When a consequential choice is unresolved, record the concrete proposal, its
reason and affected computation. Continue independent already authorized work.
Do not infer approval from a template, timeout or missing input.
