## Participants and Data

Resting-state EEG from 20 participants (subjects 1–20) of the PhysioNet EEG Motor
Movement/Imagery Database (Schalk et al., 2004), recorded with the BCI2000 system
(64 channels, international 10–10 montage). Two ~61 s baseline runs per participant were
analysed: eyes-open (R01) and eyes-closed (R02).

## Preprocessing

Channel labels were standardized and the international 10–05 montage applied. Continuous
data were resampled from 160 Hz to 250 Hz and re-referenced to the common average of all
scalp electrodes. Ocular, muscular and cardiac artifacts were removed by independent
component analysis (extended Infomax, 15 components, random seed 42) fitted on a 1 Hz
high-pass copy, with components classified by ICLabel (mne-icalabel 0.9.0) and those
labelled eye-blink, muscle, or heart removed. Analysis-specific band-pass filters
(zero-phase FIR) were then applied: 1–45 Hz for spectral analysis, 2–20 Hz for microstates,
and 0.5–45 Hz for complexity.

## Spectral power

Continuous data were segmented into 2 s fixed-length epochs. Power spectral density was
estimated with Welch's method (2 s windows, n_fft = 2·sfreq, fmin 1 Hz, fmax 45 Hz).
Posterior alpha power (8–13 Hz) was averaged over occipito-parietal electrodes
(O1/Oz/O2/PO3/PO4/POz/PO7/PO8/P-line). The individual alpha frequency and the aperiodic
(1/f) exponent (specparam 2.0.0rc7) were available but the validation headline is the
posterior alpha contrast.

## Complexity

For each channel of the broadband (0.5–45 Hz) recording we computed Lempel–Ziv complexity
(median binarization, length-normalized) and permutation entropy (order 3, normalized)
with `antropy` 0.2.2, then averaged across channels.

## Microstates

On the eyes-closed (2–20 Hz) data, microstate maps were derived by modified k-means
(pycrostates 0.6.1, K = 4, seed 42) on global-field-power peaks and back-fitted to the
continuous recording with temporal smoothing (factor 10, half-window 8 samples,
minimum segment 5 samples). Global explained variance and per-map mean durations were
computed.

## Statistics

State (eyes-closed vs eyes-open) effects were tested across the 20 participants with paired
t-tests (second level). For posterior alpha, a spatial cluster-based permutation test
(5000 permutations, seed 42, channel adjacency from the montage) controlled the family-wise
error across the 64 electrodes.

## Software

MNE-Python 1.12.1, mne-icalabel 0.9.0, pycrostates 0.6.1, antropy 0.2.2, specparam 2.0.0rc7,
scikit-learn 1.8.0, NumPy 2.4.6, SciPy 1.17.1, Python 3.11. All random seeds fixed at 42.

**Limitations.** eegbci is natively 160 Hz (below the 250 Hz the resting recipes assume) and
provides only ~61 s per state (below the recommended ≥120 s); data were therefore upsampled,
and microstate parameters in particular should be read as a functional check rather than a
stability estimate.
