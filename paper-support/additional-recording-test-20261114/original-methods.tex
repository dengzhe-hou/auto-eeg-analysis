\subsection{Additional-recording test and engineering follow-up}

The 120-session test retained the 8 November repair snapshot and evaluated 20 recording--task combinations from the same 19 task families. Five eyes-closed Mumtaz 2016 recordings from the healthy group and five EEG During Mental Arithmetic Tasks recordings were chosen by ascending identifier before new reference values or model outputs were available. Each recording supplied two requests. An inaccessible Mumtaz H S18 file was replaced by H S21 with researcher approval before execution. The protocol, schedule and resource snapshot were frozen before the first session. Models, scientific plans, numerical environment, paired allocations and acceptance rules matched the repair retest. The result-field endpoint and complete contract remained separate. Effects averaged three paired differences within each request and then weighted the 20 requests equally. Approximate 95\,\% intervals used the same 10,000-draw crossed task-family/recording procedure and seed 20261029. The positive-effect and positive-lower-endpoint criterion was retained.

The subsequent engineering retest addressed the two affected requests with six fresh sessions per condition. Both received the original scientific settings in explicit machine-readable form. The AEA condition additionally received the repaired entry and relevant procedures, including the existing common-template sample-entropy helper. Settings remained Haiku 5.5, disabled thinking, medium effort and \mne{} 1.8.0, with 1 CPU and 4 GiB per condition. The unchanged independent verifier checked first-delivery results and arrays. Actual function traces, executed code, saved source copies and successful self-checks established entry use. No model judge or interval estimation was used. These records were neither pooled with nor substituted for earlier deliveries.
