# Analysis details retained with the AEA manuscript

This fixed snapshot preserves the complete nine-page supporting document that accompanied the manuscript before the 9 November 2026 reallocation into the main paper and repository. Important analysis parameters, endpoint definitions, comparative results and tested-version information now appear in the main paper. Detailed comparisons, recording lists, workflow records and historical checks remain accessible here.

The reallocation changes presentation only. It does not rerun models, EEG processing, scoring or interval calculations, and it does not change scientific plans, expected answers or tolerances.

## Complete retained document

[analysis-details.pdf](analysis-details.pdf) preserves all nine original supporting pages, including all six notes, ten tables, the task-family and recording figure, and the references. [analysis-details.tex](analysis-details.tex) preserves the corresponding original source fragment. Its internal references and figure paths refer to the former combined manuscript and are not a standalone build project.

[task-family-and-recording-detail.pdf](task-family-and-recording-detail.pdf) is the original vector figure. It retains every task family and recording, including negative and zero differences.

## Notes in the preserved PDF

- S1 gives the approved ERP inputs, recorded N400 case, fixed no-ICA example and raw-data pilots.
- S2–S4 give numerical conventions, component comparisons, unresolved MMN differences and adapted N170 checks.
- S5–S7 give the new-recording study's inputs, task coverage, objective tolerances, endpoint definitions and cost accounting.
- S7–S8 give repair and final ERP comparisons, including earlier-configuration outcomes, saved-score replay and limitations of the degenerate empirical intervals.
- S8–S9 give tested versions and references.

The original main-paper [workflow-output table](tables/main-workflow-outputs.tex), [comparison overview](tables/main-comparison-overview.tex) and [first-delivery cost table](tables/main-first-delivery-and-costs.tex) are also retained. Their essential information has been integrated into the main text or its numerical tables.

## Scope and access

These are configuration-specific analysis and engineering records. Adapted examples, numerical comparisons and model evaluations remain separate forms of evidence. Development-seen ERP requests and reused continuous inputs do not establish transfer to unseen recordings.

This directory contains no raw third-party EEG, restricted SEED-V signal arrays, private access links or credentials. Original provider access and licence conditions still apply. Repository software licensing and each source record's stated provenance remain unchanged.
